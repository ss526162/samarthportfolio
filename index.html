05 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

What kinds of businesses have you worked with?

My experience includes B2B, SaaS, healthcare, education, home services, automotive and local businesses across the USA, Australia, Canada and India.

Can you work on both SEO and Google Ads?

Yes. My experience covers organic and local SEO alongside Search, Display, Local and Demand Gen campaigns. I also implement GA4 and GTM tracking to connect activity with leads and other useful outcomes.

What do GEO and AEO mean?

Generative Engine Optimization and Answer Engine Optimization focus on making content clear, useful and understandable for AI-powered search and answer experiences. My work includes entity clarity, structured data and topical relevance. No placement or ranking is guaranteed.

Can you guarantee a number-one Google ranking?

No. Rankings depend on competition, website quality and search systems outside anyone’s control. I focus on sound technical foundations, useful content and measurable improvements rather than guaranteed positions.

What should I include when getting in touch?

Share your website, target market, primary goal and any current SEO or ad campaigns. That gives us a useful starting point for discussing the work.
